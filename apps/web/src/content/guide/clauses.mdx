A resolution is built from two kinds of clauses. The full vocabulary list is
in the clauses PDF under [Documents](#downloads).

## Preambulatory clauses

Set out *why* the committee is acting: past resolutions, treaties, and the
facts of the problem. They end in a comma and are written in italics.

*Affirming* · *Alarmed by* · *Bearing in mind* · *Deeply concerned* ·
*Noting with regret* · *Recalling*

## Operative clauses

Set out *what* the committee will do. They are numbered, underlined at the
start, and end in a semicolon — the last one in a full stop.

Calls upon · Decides · Encourages · Requests · Urges · Further recommends

## Structure at a glance

> Committee: [ECOSOC] · Topic: [topic title] · Submitted by: [delegation]
>
> The Economic and Social Council,
>
> *Recalling* [the relevant resolution or treaty],
>
> *Deeply concerned* by [the problem being addressed],
>
> *Noting* [the relevant fact or report],
>
> 1\. Calls upon [who does what];
>
> 2\. Requests [the follow-up action];
>
> 3\. Decides [to remain seized of the matter].

The bracketed slots are for you to fill in — start from the resolution
template in [Documents](#downloads) rather than a blank page.
