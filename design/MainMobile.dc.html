01 — About
What is DAEMUN?

DAEMUN is a student-led Model United Nations where students explore various issues in the international community and seek practical and implementable solutions.

Through discussions and collaboration, DAEMUN provides participants with opportunities to develop critical thinking skills, diplomatic communication abilities, and global leadership.

03 — Theme
“From Vulnerability to Voice” emphasizes multilateral cooperation in developing isolated vulnerabilities into voices for dialogue, solidarity, and change.

Students will be respecting diverse perspectives, representing vulnerable communities, and exploring how even a small voice can result in meaningful transformations.
